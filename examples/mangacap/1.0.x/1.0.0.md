## How to update
- `git pull origin main`
- Nothing to re-run: the renderer changes only affect future `mangacap cut`
  invocations.

## 🔍 Research 🔍

* Frame study of three top anti-recap caption styles (30fps stepped through
  letter by letter at 3× zoom): survivable captions are one line, dead
  center, thick-stroked, and never anchored to a moving panel edge.
* Safe-zone measurement across TikTok/Shorts/Reels UI overlays: captions
  between 38% and 62% of frame height never collide with platform chrome.

## 🧬 General 🧬
### Added

* `mangacap cut chapter-12 --voice nova` — the full pipeline: panel pairing,
  narration script fetch, TTS synthesis, caption burn, vertical render at
  1080×1920/30fps.
* `--dry-run` prints the cut plan (panel ranges, caption blocks, per-entry
  durations) without spending TTS credits.
* `timing.json` next to every render: entry → audio duration covered beats;
  the audio is the sync clock and the plan is reproducible from it.

### Changed

* Panel pairing favors dialogue bubbles that escape their panel — the reader
  assigns spillover text to the nearest art panel instead of cropping a
  bubble-only strip.

## 🎞️ Renderer 🎞️
### Added

* Caption blocks are width-measured against the chosen font at render scale
  and never wrap — an over-long block splits on the last comma instead.
* Every entry's audio duration carries a configurable tail pad
  (`--pad <seconds>`, default 0.2) so beats don't butt together.
